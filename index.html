CartelBench

Do LLM market makers collude — and can anything stop them? A benchmark that drops language models into ABIDES, JPMorgan's order-book simulator, as market maker, trader or regulator, scored only on the order book and the cash ledger.

Told to run a cartel, models comply and take real money off captured retail flow. Left alone, none initiates. Invited, it turns entirely on how persuasive the invitation is. Three earlier claims were withdrawn under adversarial review.

My contribution

Reproducibility and artifact engineering — one-command regeneration of every table and figure, CI gates rewritten to fail closed, a double-blind anonymisation scrub, and the ACM acmart build with an enforced page budget.
